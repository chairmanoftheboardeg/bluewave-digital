The Methodology

Timeline Of How We Work

1

Contact & Outreach

Reach out to us via any official platforms (preferably through our website form). Our agents will engage with you at the earliest availability.

2

Assessment & Quotation

We assess the scope of your request and formulate a precise project quote. This will include a preliminary project charge varying between 100 to 8,000 SCR, depending entirely on the project's scale.

3

The Execution Phase

Once the preliminary charge is successfully processed, our engineering and design teams initiate the build.

  • Design Phase

4

Client Review

Once testing has ended, our agents contact you for a thorough review. This can be conducted onsite or remotely via call/text. If you are satisfied, we proceed to launch. If dissatisfied, we roll back to the Design phase and repeat until expectations are met.
